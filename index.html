How it works

1 · Coil

Realised volatility (ATR/close) sits in the bottom 40% of its trailing year — price is compressed and quiet.

2 · Break

Price closes beyond the prior 40-bar high or low.

3 · Confirm

Volume must clear 2× its 20-bar average. Flat-volume breaks are marked as fakeouts, not signals.

4 · Manage

A 3×ATR trailing stop from the best close since entry, plus a 10-bar time stop. Fully mechanical.

FAQ

Does it repaint?

No. Every input uses only completed bars — confirmed by an automated lookahead audit during validation. Signals appear at bar close and never move.

What markets does it work on?

The validated evidence is Binance crypto perpetuals on daily bars. The indicator runs on any symbol/timeframe in TradingView, but we make no claims outside the tested setting — and say so up front.

How many signals will I see?

About two per month per chart at canonical settings. It is deliberately selective; if you want more frequency, the honest path is re-validation on your market, not loosening the filters.

Why show the forward expectation as only 0.4–0.7 Sharpe?
